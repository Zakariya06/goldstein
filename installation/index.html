Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to your drain problems.

Clear guidance before we get to work.

What makes water back up in the first place?+

Water backs up because grease, wipes, roots, scale buildup, damage, or a blockage further down the line restricts flow and forces water back to the nearest outlet.

Can a line be put right without digging up the ground?+

Often, yes. We inspect the pipe first, then recommend trenchless cleaning or repair when the line can be restored without opening the ground.

How long is somebody normally on site for?+

Most drain repairs take 1 to 3 hours, depending on access and the cause. We explain the work and timing before we begin.

What changes when the trouble sits out in the sewer rather than inside the house?+

A sewer-side issue usually needs a longer camera inspection and a different repair plan. We locate the break or root intrusion before discussing the next step.
