How it works

Water Managed Outside.
Basement Stays Dry.

  1. Rain & snow soak in Rainfall and melting snow naturally filter into the soil around your home.
  2. Gravel creates a path Water moves through the gravel bed, flowing toward the perforated drain tile.
  3. Water enters the pipe The perforated pipe collects groundwater and carries it away from your foundation.
  4. Redirected away Water is safely discharged away from your home, keeping pressure off your walls and basement.

How We Install Drain Tile
The Right Way

A proven process. Care in every step. Protection that lasts.

  1. 1

    Inspect

    We camera the existing drain tile to locate blockages, breaks, or collapsed sections.

  2. 2

    Trench

    Carefully excavate along the foundation to expose the footing and create a clean work area.

  3. 3

    Install

    Lay new perforated pipe on a bed of clean stone for maximum flow and protection.

  4. 4

    Wrap & cover

    Wrap in non-woven filter fabric and cover with stone to keep silt out and water moving.

  5. 5

    Backfill & restore

    Backfill, compact, and restore the area - your yard looks the way we found it.

Frequently asked questions

What Homeowners Ask
Before a Drain Tile Install

A few straight answers make the whole job easier to plan.

How do I know the old perimeter line has given up?+

Recurring damp walls, musty odours, water after heavy rain, or a camera inspection showing crushed and blocked pipe are common signs the old system is no longer moving water properly.

How many days is a house normally torn up for?+

Timing depends on access, foundation length, soil conditions, and repair scope. After inspection, we provide a clear schedule and explain which areas will be open each day.

What happens to lawn, shrubs, and paving while machinery is on site?+

We protect what matters. Access paths are planned, landscaping is carefully worked around, and surfaces are restored once the system is in. You get the benefit without the long-term mess.

What upkeep keeps everything running years later?+

Keep gutters, downspouts, and surface drains clear, direct roof water away from the foundation, and arrange periodic camera inspection or flushing when flow begins to slow.
